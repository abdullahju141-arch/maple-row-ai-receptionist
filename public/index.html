    How it works

    A real conversation, a real booking

    1. You talk

      Your voice streams from the browser to the clinic's server, the same way a phone line would.

    2. Ava understands

      Google Gemini Live listens and replies in a natural voice. You can interrupt her, just like a person.

    3. The calendar decides

      When Ava needs a time, the server checks the appointment book and saves the booking. She can't double-book or invent a slot.
